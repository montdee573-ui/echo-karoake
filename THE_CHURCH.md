# THE CHURCH

The Church is the intelligence architecture powering the Echo Karaoke ecosystem.

Its purpose is to observe, measure, analyze, score, and optimize live vocal performances in real time.

The Church operates as a collection of specialized units working together to transform karaoke into a competitive skill-based entertainment platform.
THE CHURCH

├── Echo Music Rights Unit
├── Performance Initialization Unit
├── Timing Unit
├── Nodes Unit
├── Echo Monitor
├── Weight Engine
├── Echo Score Engine
├── Owner AI Unit
├── Session Engine
├── Payment Engine
├── Photography Unit
├── Signature Moments Unit
└── Echo Sounds Monitor
